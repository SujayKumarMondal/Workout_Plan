Mon → Sat

Six days of progressive training.

Keep most working sets about 1–3 reps shy of failure. Add reps first, then small amounts of weight when form is stable.

🔥 BEFORE • 8–10 min

5 min treadmill/cycle + joint circles + 1–2 light warm-up sets of the first lift.

🏋️ DURING • 60–90 min

Controlled reps, full comfortable range, rest 60–120 sec. No need for 2-hour sessions.

🧘 AFTER • 5–10 min

Easy walking/cycling + relaxed breathing + gentle stretches. Rehydrate and eat your post-workout meal.

Chest & Triceps

Build pressing strength and chest/arm shape while keeping volume recoverable.

Optional: incline walk 10–15 min

Target areas: CHEST + TRICEPS

Primary: chest and triceps. Assists: front shoulders.

Weeks 1-2

Choose loads that leave 2-3 good reps in reserve; use the listed rep ranges with controlled form.

Progression

Add reps first. Once every set reaches the top of its range cleanly twice, add the smallest available load.

Lower dumbbells under control; press up without crashing them together.","incline")} ${ex("Pec Deck Fly","3 × 12–15","Keep a soft elbow bend; bring handles together by squeezing the chest, not by swinging.","fly")} ${ex("Cable Triceps Pushdown","3 × 10–15","Elbows stay close to the torso; extend forearms while shoulders remain quiet.","pushdown")} ${ex("Dumbbell Overhead Triceps Extension","2 × 10–15","Brace core, elbows point forward; lower dumbbell behind head and extend smoothly.","overhead")}

Back & Biceps

Prioritize back width, thickness and arm development.

Optional: easy cycling 10 min

Target areas: BACK + BICEPS

Primary: lats, mid-back and biceps. Assists: rear shoulders and forearms.

Weeks 1-2

Use a full, comfortable stretch and a brief squeeze; keep torso position steady on every pull.

Progression

Build reps to the top of each range, then raise the load slightly without swinging or shortening the motion.

${ex("Lat Pulldown","3 × 8–12","Pull the bar toward the upper chest with elbows down; do not yank behind the neck.","pulldown")} ${ex("Seated Cable Row","3 × 8–12","Neutral spine; pull handles toward the lower ribs and squeeze shoulder blades.","row")} ${ex("Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row","3 × 10–12","Chest stays supported; drive elbows back and pause briefly at the top.","chestrow")} ${ex("Barbell Biceps Curl","3 × 10–12","Keep elbows near your sides and avoid leaning back to move the bar.","curl")} ${ex("Hammer Curl","2 × 10–12","Neutral grip; curl without swinging and lower slowly.","hammer")}

Legs

Build quads, hamstrings, glutes and calves for a balanced athletic base.

Optional: easy treadmill 10 min

Target areas: LEGS

Primary: quads, hamstrings, glutes and calves.

Weeks 1-2

Find a stable, pain-free range of motion and control both the lowering and lifting phases.

Progression

Add reps before load. Increase weight only after all sets reach the top of their range with steady balance and no discomfort.

${ex("Goblet Squat","3 × 10–12","Hold dumbbell close to chest; sit between the hips and drive up through the floor.","squat")} ${ex("Leg Press","3 × 10–12","Feet shoulder-width; lower platform under control without letting the lower back round.","legpress")} ${ex("Leg Extension","3 × 10–15","Align knee with machine pivot; extend smoothly and pause briefly at the top.","extension")} ${ex("Leg Curl","3 × 10–15","Keep hips down; curl heels toward glutes and control the return.","curlleg")} ${ex("Standing Calf Raise","3 × 12–15","Full comfortable stretch at bottom, rise high on the balls of the feet, pause.","calf")}

Shoulders & Abs

Build shoulder width and core control without endless ab work.

Optional: 10–15 min incline walk

Target areas: SHOULDERS + ABS

Primary: front, side and rear shoulders; abs and deep core.

Weeks 1-2

Keep ribs stacked over hips; use a comfortable shoulder range and stop core reps before form slips.

Progression

Add clean reps to raises and crunches; build plank time gradually, then add only a small resistance increase.

The full system

Muscular lifestyle, not just gym time.

The physique comes from what you repeat outside the gym too: sleep, steps, hydration, food quality, recovery and progression.

😴

Sleep

Day shift: aim 11:00 PM–6:30 AM. Night shift: aim roughly 5:30 AM–1:30 PM. Dark, cool room; consistent timing; caffeine cutoff ~6–8 hours before sleep.

💧

Water

Start around 2.5–3 L/day, more when sweating heavily. Drink steadily rather than forcing huge amounts at once. Pale-yellow urine is a practical hydration cue.

🚶

Steps

Build toward 7,000–10,000 steps/day. On exhausting workdays, consistency matters more than hitting an exact number.

🍚

Food quality

Base meals around eggs, fish, chicken, soy, dal, curd/milk, rice/roti, oats, fruit and cheap seasonal vegetables. Limit fried snacks, sugary drinks and frequent takeout.

📈

Progressive overload

Track reps and weights. When you reach the top of the rep range with clean form, increase the load slightly next time.

🧠

Recovery

Sunday is recovery. Easy walking and mobility are fine. Do not “make up” missed workouts by doubling volume the next day.

When to adjust calories

If weight/waist is not trending down for 2–4 consistent weeks, first check portions, liquid calories, steps and sleep. If everything is genuinely consistent, make a small calorie adjustment rather than a crash cut. If strength is collapsing, recovery is poor or hunger is extreme, reassess the deficit.
